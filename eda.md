# Brief Summary

- Overall the data is pretty clean with not that many NA values.

- From initial analysis, it seems that most kickstarters failed (168221 failed and 113081 successful)

- It is also not unusual for kickstarters to be canceled (32354 canceled)

- It seems that campaign lentgh is doesn't have too much spread.
    - Mean is at 34 days
    - 25th percentile is 30 and 75th percentile is 36 days
    - Outliers are at 90 days and 1 day. (Assuming that was canceled)

- It seems that campaign lentgh and sucess of the kickstarter is not correlated.

- It looks like the lower the goal a kickstarter set, the higher the chance of success was for them. 
    - This makes sense since it may be easier to raise less money than more.

- It seems that the month that a kickstarter launches doesn't effect its success.
